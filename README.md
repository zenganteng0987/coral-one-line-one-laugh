Entry for the [One Line, One Laugh](https://make.fun/competitions/u-coral-404-one-line-one-laugh-22f2c9f3) competition on make.fun.

`main.ts` writes a single hilarious line of at most 200 characters to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it to stdout. A judge model compares the lines two at a time and the funniest line takes the reward.

## The line

> My fridge called my therapist. Turns out opening it at 3am, staring into the light, and eating nothing is a cry for help.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```
